Earlyward

Earlyward is a free, open-source WordPress plugin. It looks for signs of an attack, spots hardening that is missing or broken, and explains hosting and runtime problems in plain language: what is wrong, why it matters, how sure it is, and the exact steps to fix it. Every finding shows its evidence (file and line, user, option or URL) so you can judge it yourself.

What it checks

Earlyward organizes findings into distinct diagnostic areas. Findings provide confidence ratings and concrete remediation guidance:

Runtime and outage

Detects silent homepage outages against a known-good baseline, fatal error recovery states, ballooning error logs, and stalled background cron jobs.

Hosting

Flags permissions on critical files, account isolation weaknesses across neighbor sites, pending updates, disk exhaustion, and outdated PHP versions.

Malware indicators

Identifies unauthorized PHP in content and upload folders, hidden prepend or append loaders, automation-like administrator accounts, and cloaked content.

Known vulnerabilities (opt-in)

Optional matching of installed plugins, themes, and WordPress versions against a public vulnerability feed on your own server.

Scans never write; fixes need your confirmation

Earlyward adheres to a strict principle of safety and user control:

  • Scans never write: Routine scans in WP-Cron or via WP-CLI are read-only. Scans do not alter your database content or modify files.
  • Fixes need your confirmation: Any change requires an administrator to inspect the finding, tick a confirmation box, and click the fix action. Fixes re-verify the finding in real time before acting.
  • Reversible quarantine: Suspicious files are moved into a restricted, locked folder rather than deleted outright, allowing them to be restored if needed.
  • Complete override: Defining EARLYWARD_NO_FIXES in wp-config.php disables all fix actions and removes all fix buttons.

Emergency off switch

To deactivate Earlyward completely at any moment:

define( 'EARLYWARD_DISABLE', true );

Adding this line to wp-config.php or deleting the wp-content/plugins/earlyward folder immediately halts all plugin activity.
